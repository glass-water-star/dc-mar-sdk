# Contributor Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant v2.1. We expect contributors to be respectful and inclusive.

Our standards include:

- Be respectful and considerate in language and actions.
- Avoid demeaning or exclusionary language and behavior.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, and discrimination.

To report a violation, please open an issue or contact the maintainers via the repository on GitHub. Reports will be handled promptly and confidentially.

Thank you for helping us keep the community welcoming.
